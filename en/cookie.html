What cookies are

Cookies are small files that websites save on a visitor's device. Some are essential for the site to work, others are used to measure how it is used or to show personalised advertising.

Technical cookies

These are necessary for the site to work and require no consent. On this site they are limited to two preferences stored in your browser, which are never sent to us:

  • Chosen language — so you are not asked again on every visit.
  • Reading position — so you resume where you left off when reloading the page.
