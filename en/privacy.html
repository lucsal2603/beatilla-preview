What data this website collects

This website was built to collect as little as possible. It uses no analytics, profiling or advertising systems, installs no tracking cookies and shares no data with third parties. Even the typefaces are hosted on our own server, so simply visiting the site makes no request to any external service.

The “Check availability” form

The form on this site sends nothing to our servers: filling it in opens your own email application with the message already written, which stays on your device until you decide to send it. We receive your email only once you press send.

The data you send us in that message (name, email address, dates of stay and any requests) is processed in order to reply to you and handle your booking enquiry. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual measures at your request (art. 6.1.b GDPR). We keep the correspondence for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and, where a stay takes place, for the tax and administrative obligations required by law.

Automatically collected data

As with any website, the hosting service automatically records some technical browsing data (IP address, date and time of the request, browser type). It is used solely to deliver the site and keep it secure, and never to identify individual visitors.

Cookies and browser storage

This site uses no profiling cookies and therefore requires no consent banner. A single technical preference is stored in your browser — the language you chose (Italian or English) — so you are not asked again on every visit. It stays on your device, is never sent to us, and you can delete it by clearing your browser data.

Bookings and external services

The Book now button leads to the Wubook booking platform, and the map at the bottom of the page is provided by OpenStreetMap. These are external sites with their own privacy notices: any data entered there is handled under their respective policies.

Your rights

At any time you may request access to your data, its correction, erasure, restriction of processing or object to it, as well as request its portability. Simply write to info@agriturismobeatilla.it. If you believe the processing breaches the regulation, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Changes

Any updates to this notice will be published on this page, together with the date of the latest revision.
